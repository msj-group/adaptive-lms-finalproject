What this workspace contains

This milestone builds the consent foundation only. There is no interaction tracking, no experiment, no session recording, no survey, no rating, no export and no model. Nothing about a student's behaviour is collected, measured or inferred here.

A participant shown as Active has read and accepted a specific version of the consent document. A participant shown as Withdrawn is no longer eligible for any future research session. Consent wording, data retention and the treatment of previously collected data still require ethics approval before any real research collection begins.
