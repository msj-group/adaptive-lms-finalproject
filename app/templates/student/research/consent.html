What the study would look at

The research looks at how students move through learning activities, in order to study behavioural patterns associated with possible frustration. The platform does not know, and does not claim to know, how any student feels.

No research data is being collected at this stage. Nothing is recorded about what you type, no keystrokes, no passwords or form contents, no audio, no camera and no browser fingerprinting — not now and not as part of this consent. Your messages, your fees and your grades are never part of any research collection. How long any future research data would be kept, and what would happen to data collected before a withdrawal, still require ethics approval and are not decided here.
