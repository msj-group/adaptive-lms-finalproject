What this platform records, and what it does not

What is recorded. That you were invited to take part, and — once you submit one — the decision you submit: which consent document version you read, a digest of its exact wording, and the server date and time. Accepting, declining and withdrawing each add one such record to your consent history.

What is not collected. This milestone collects no behavioural interaction data: no keystrokes, no typing content, no mouse or scroll tracking, no screen or audio recording, no camera and no browser fingerprinting. It runs no experiment sessions or task sessions, and holds no surveys, frustration ratings, observer annotations, research exports, datasets, trained models or inference of any kind.

Everything else is in the consent document. What the study looks at, what would be collected, how long anything is kept, the terms for withdrawing and what happens to your information afterwards are stated in the consent document, never on this page — so the wording your decision is recorded against is the wording that answers those questions.
