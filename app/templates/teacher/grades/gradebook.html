A group's grade is split into weighted categories that must add up to exactly 100%. Each category holds grade items — an assignment, a quiz or listening activity, a speaking activity, a class activity, or a grade you enter yourself. Creating an item captures the students enrolled at that moment, and that list never changes afterwards. Students see nothing until you release an item; after that you can still correct a score, and the student sees the correction.
