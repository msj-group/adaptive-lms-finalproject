# Centre operation of Version A

Use the HTTPS address supplied by the centre's hosting operator. Sign in on the
preserved Youth Centre Login. Your saved role opens its own workspace; users
cannot choose a higher role. Keep accounts personal, sign out on shared devices,
and use Account settings for your password/profile photo. Display controls
offer Light/Dark/System, language, density and reduced motion. On a phone,
Students use Home, Courses, Activities, Search and More; all other destinations
remain in More. Other roles use the mobile navigation menu.

## Initial centre setup

The hosting operator deliberately creates the first Administrator and optional
approved Researcher in a private terminal. The centre receives no shared demo
passwords or imported Development/Pilot records. Ask the operator to verify
hosting smoke, backup and daily retention before real use.

The Administrator creates academic terms, levels and courses with reviewed
prices; then groups, rooms and schedules; then Teacher/Student accounts and
group memberships. Follow the existing active/archive and enrollment workflows.
Historical records are preserved. Review warnings and confirmation screens;
they describe consequences of existing business rules. Provisioned temporary
passwords should be delivered privately and changed through Account settings.

## Student and Teacher use

Students open their current courses, outline and lessons, download available
materials and mark lessons complete. That progress records self-marked published
lessons; it is not a proficiency diagnosis. Activities retain separate Assignment,
Quiz, Listening and Speaking rules. Save Listening answers explicitly. Quiz
autosave confirms the saved state; do not assume an answer was saved during an
unavailable connection. Confirm final submission only when ready.

The Teacher configures each Assignment as TEXT or SINGLE FILE; the Student
cannot switch it. A file task accepts one private allowed file within its shown
size/type limit. Submission is final: no draft, replacement, multiple files or
resubmission. A failed file attempt requires selecting the file again. A receipt
and authorized download prove the accepted submission. The assigned Teacher
reviews it and writes feedback. Feedback is a comment, not an official grade.
Gradebook release remains a separate Teacher action; score corrections preserve
revision history. Attendance draft saving and finalization are separate actions.
Finalization exposes saved marks and ends Teacher editing; permitted Admin
corrections preserve the original values/history.

Messages retain Inbox, new conversation/recipient/subject, reply, edit, hide,
clear and Emoji picker. Mobile action sheets present the same existing actions.
There is no typing/presence/read-receipt/realtime promise. Follow the warnings
on clear/hide; your action does not give permission to modify another person's
history. Announcements, discussions, calendar and notifications use their
existing permissions/routes/order.

## Financial operation

Money forms record actual cash or verified/pending bank movements under existing
rules. No online provider or simulated checkout is enabled. Invoice context
relates a movement without allocating installments. A positive balance is due
from the Student; a negative balance is credit owed to the Student. Pending
collections do not change balance. Preview writes nothing; server save validates
again. Corrections/deletions/cancellations retain original documents and audit
history. Verify the amount, method and current record before confirmation.

## Errors and unavailable resources

Review field errors and the error summary. A page with no records may be empty
because nothing has been created or your access/window has ended. Do not repeat
final transactions blindly. Check the saved receipt/current record first.
If media/files cannot load, retry or use the existing download link; notify the
operator if it persists. For a general error, record the displayed reference
and approximate time without copying message/password/answer content into logs.

## Research operation

The separate Researcher workspace shows collection configurations and status,
pseudonymous sessions/timelines, raw optional 1–5 ratings, coverage/missingness,
delivery counters, storage and exports. Each new analytic chart declares its
scope/source/time and denominator where applicable. Missing ratings are not zero.
Raw self-report is not a psychological diagnosis or causal/ML result.

Collection requires an active collecting configuration, current collection
period, eligible active Student and no exclusion. Centre participation/consent
arrangements remain external; automatic subject creation does not record consent.
Keep approved sampling, windows, Rating and retention unchanged. Passwords,
answer/message/subject text, recipient names and uploaded content/names are
outside research metadata. An Account form attempt does not imply success.

Before real collection the hosting operator must complete the production smoke
and the approved Study transition described in `VERSION_A_RESEARCH_OPERATIONS.md`.
Setup, previews, browser checks and Development/Pilot data never become Study.

To check collection: inspect the current configuration period and collecting
state; verify recent eligible sessions and accepted events, current dictionary,
delivery-invalid/drop counters and retention success. No recent data is not proof
of a fault when nobody eligible used the platform. Browser buffer losses are
only partially observable. To stop safely, use the existing Pause/Stop collection
action, confirm status and a subsequent eligible page has no collector. Business
use continues. Export only the intended configuration/date scope; review manifest,
counts, hashes and dataset classification, then store the archive privately.

## Operator responsibilities

The hosting operator owns startup/restart, environment/secrets, MySQL/private-file
backups and restore, TLS, scheduler/logs and deployment diagnostics. The centre
does not need Codex or engineering workflow for ordinary UI use. Keep the named
operator contact with the HTTPS address and backup/retention schedule. DB and
private files must be backed up together. Restore is a controlled operator
procedure; users should not reset the database. Once real centre/research data
exists, the development reset authorization no longer applies.
